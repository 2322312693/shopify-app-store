## App introduction

## App details

Create original logo concepts for products, collections, and campaigns. Enter a brand name and creative direction, then generate a square logo inside Shopify admin. Optionally upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP reference to guide a substantially redesigned concept. Preview and download the result, or add it as a new product image while preserving existing media.

## Testing instructions

1. Install the app on a Shopify development store and open it from Shopify admin.
2. On the app home page, enter `Northstar Coffee` as the brand name.
3. Enter `Minimal mountain symbol, deep green and warm gold, premium outdoor feel` as the creative direction.
4. Click `Generate logo concept`. The app opens a dedicated result page. A successful generation uses one logo from the plan; a failed generation is refunded.
5. Click `Download logo` to verify download behavior, then use `Back to logo creator` to return to the creation page.
6. If the store has a product, select it under `Add logo to product`, then click `Add as new product image`. Confirm that the logo is added and existing media remains unchanged.
7. To test reference-guided generation, upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP image no larger than 5 MB, enter a new direction, and generate again.
8. Open `View plans / Manage subscription` to verify Shopify-managed pricing. Development stores may display test pricing and do not create a real payout.

The app requests only `read_products` and `write_products`. It does not request customer or order data.

Reference image bytes upload directly from the merchant browser to Cloudflare R2 by a short-lived signed URL. The app server receives only the resulting image URL, which prevents large uploads from exceeding the hosting request-body limit.
